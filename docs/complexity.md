## The Architecture of Complexity

1. coupling - conflating different concerns

Fix: separation of concerns

2. cohesion - separating what must be unified

Fix: identify the interfaces to unify otherwise different system/domain

3. core/specific split

Fix: Identify the common denominator to core, split the differences to its specific domain
